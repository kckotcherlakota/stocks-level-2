Version 1.0 | Review date: November 11, 2026 | Scope: long calls and
puts; intraday U.S. equities / ETFs

DECISION: APPROVE FOR PAPER-TRADING RESEARCH ONLY — NOT VALIDATED FOR
LIVE AUTONOMOUS TRADING.

Purpose. Convert the supplied price-action + session VWAP + 9/20 EMA +
RSI + ATR + time-of-day RVOL + 15-minute opening range + optional
SPY/QQQ SMT framework into measurable, auditable signals and execution
controls. No profitability or “high probability” claim is established
without out-of-sample, option-level testing.

Evidence note. Academic and working-paper studies report promising ORB
results for specific tests/universes; this does not establish that these
exact 15-minute, ETF, earnings-stock, or options rules are profitable
[1–2].

2 | Canonical definitions (must be fixed before testing)

-   Clock: all strategy windows use America/New_York; regular U.S. stock
    session 9:30–16:00 ET on normal trading days. Holiday and
    early-close calendars override defaults.

-   Primary signal: completed 5-minute underlying bars, adjusted
    consistently for splits/dividends where applicable. Intrabar
    triggers are not counted as bar-close signals.

-   VWAP: cumulative session price × volume / cumulative volume, reset
    at 9:30 ET; document bar approximation (e.g., typical price) versus
    trade-level calculation. Exclude premarket unless the specific
    experiment says otherwise.

-   EMAs: EMA(9) and EMA(20) on 5-minute underlying closes. Rising means
    current EMA > EMA N completed bars ago (N initially 3; tune only in
    training).

-   ATR: 14 completed 5-minute true ranges (including previous-close gap
    handling as defined in code). ATR stops are buffers or alternative
    candidates; never move a stop farther away after entry without a
    separate tested rule.

-   RVOL at time t: cumulative regular-session volume through t divided
    by median cumulative volume through the same t across the prior 20
    eligible sessions. Median is a robust proposed choice; compare with
    mean as an experiment. No future-session data.

-   Opening range high/low: maximum/minimum underlying price in
    9:30–9:45 ET, frozen at 9:45. Trigger bars begin after range
    completion.

-   SMT: bearish if one ETF exceeds a prior same-window confirmed swing
    high while the other does not; bullish analogous lows. Fix swing
    lookback (initially 3 bars each side) and confirmation delay. No
    future pivot knowledge.

-   R: initial planned dollar loss, calculated using executable option
    entry estimate and conservative estimated liquidation value at
    underlying invalidation, plus fees/slippage; cannot exceed maximum
    premium at risk for a fully paid long option.

3 | Market-regime classifier (priority order)

1.  Pre-market information gate: confirm scheduled earnings, macro
    releases, corporate actions, market halts and abnormal spreads.
    Block trading if market data is stale or an event exclusion applies.

2.  Trend candidate: price stays predominantly on one side of VWAP,
    EMA(9) aligned with EMA(20), VWAP slope consistent, and no repeated
    VWAP crossings in recent bars. Thresholds must be chosen using
    training data.

3.  Failed gap candidate: gap relative to previous regular-session
    close, rejection of an opening level, and completed close back
    through VWAP with lower high/higher low.

4.  Choppy / uncertain: multiple VWAP crosses, overlapping bars,
    conflicting direction or low liquidity; default NO TRADE.

5.  Event momentum: confirmed earnings/news catalyst, unusual
    time-of-day RVOL, and clean OR breakout; apply separate model
    statistics from ETFs.

Avoid circularity: do not use the future day’s full trend/range to label
a regime at the time of entry.

4 | Fully specified strategy entry templates

A. Opening-range breakout / trend continuation

-   Long call candidate: after 9:45, completed 5m close above OR high;
    price > session VWAP; EMA9 > EMA20; positive VWAP slope; RVOL above
    pre-registered cutoff (initial research grid: 1.2, 1.5, 2.0);
    spread/liquidity pass.

-   Entry: limit order on retest of OR high that holds, or first defined
    continuation break; treat those as separate experiments. Invalidate
    on underlying return below retest swing low / OR high with ATR
    buffer, whichever predeclared rule is chosen.

-   Long put: exact directional mirror. Suppress entry when the next
    obvious target does not provide sufficient modeled reward relative
    to premium risk.

B. Gap fade / reversal

-   Gap-up fade / put: completed rejection near OR high, close below
    VWAP, confirmed lower high, then break below rejection candle low.
    Stop reference above rejection swing high plus tested ATR buffer.
    Targets: session VWAP only if not already crossed; otherwise logical
    support and prior close.

-   Gap-down fade / call: inverse. Require enough distance to target
    after options execution costs; do not fade a gap merely because it
    is large.

C. Midday VWAP first pullback

-   Time gate: 11:00–13:30 ET. Long call: established uptrend, EMA9 >
    EMA20, rising VWAP, first qualifying return to VWAP, then 5m reclaim
    close and break above trigger candle high. Swing-low / ATR-buffer
    invalidation. Inverse for puts.

-   Define “first”: zero prior completed VWAP contact events since the
    initial directional impulse; freeze contact tolerance (e.g.,
    fraction of ATR) before validation. More than 2 VWAP crosses in
    prior 12 bars => NO TRADE (research starting threshold).

D. Post-earnings ORB

-   Treat earnings as a separate stock-level dataset. Only after first
    15 minutes: closed breakout beyond OR, VWAP/EMA alignment, elevated
    same-time RVOL, executable option market and no halt. Enter on
    tested retest or continuation rule; reject excessively extended
    price versus ATR.

-   Never use SPY/QQQ SMT to claim confirmation of a single-stock
    earnings reaction without separately proving cross-asset relevance.

E. After 13:30 ET, 1DTE instrument overlay

-   Not a signal. Apply only an already-qualified strategy. Define 1DTE
    according to actual listed option expiration trading days, not
    blindly calendar days; verify chain listing and exchange calendar.

-   Approximate expected underlying move from implied volatility is NOT
    option profit. Evaluate option payoff/mark-to-model at specific
    underlying target, exit time and volatility scenarios; include
    theta, vega, gamma and spreads. Reject unattractive payoff after
    costs.

5 | SMT and momentum: controlled experiments

Baseline = price + VWAP + EMA + RVOL + setup-specific structural signal.
Compare to baseline + RSI filter; baseline + SMT filter; baseline +
both. SMT should not veto unrelated earnings names by default. Report
paired differences by market regime and month, not just aggregate win
rate.

Initial RSI research proposal (not proven): for bullish trends,
RSI(14) > 50 and rising; bearish RSI < 50 and falling. Record whether
this reduces trade count more than it improves net expectancy.

6 | Options contract selection and execution gates

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Check                               Suggested research default; adjust
                                      with evidence
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Allowed positions                   Long calls or long puts only in
                                      phase 1; one live position per
                                      symbol; no naked short option legs.

  Expiration                          Test 1DTE against 2–7DTE
                                      separately. Expiration must have
                                      tradable market at proposed exit
                                      time.

  Delta                               Test absolute delta 0.40–0.65
                                      versus alternate bands; delta is
                                      not a fixed probability of profit.

  Quote quality                       Reject zero bid,
                                      locked/crossed/stale market,
                                      abnormal spread or low depth.
                                      Example candidate threshold: spread
                                      <= 10% of mid AND <= $0.15;
                                      stress-test these.

  Order type                          Marketable limit with defined
                                      maximum debit; cancellation/reprice
                                      with a cap. Do not assume mid-price
                                      fills.

  Sizing                              Quantity = floor(min(per-trade risk
                                      budget / modeled per-contract loss,
                                      max total premium budget /
                                      per-contract debit)). Never size
                                      using price stop distance alone.

  Hard blocks                         No quote integrity, no supported
                                      contract, severe slippage, API
                                      disconnect, loss cap breached,
                                      unexpected halt, or expiration
                                      closeout uncertainty.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

For a fully paid long option, the entire premium plus trading costs can
be lost. Structural stops may execute at worse prices during gaps or
thin markets. Prefer bid-side liquidation stress scenarios when
computing projected loss.

7 | Exit engine and order of operations

6.  Safety first: stale data, broker rejects, market halt, unexpected
    exercise exposure or risk-limit violation => cancel new entries;
    initiate broker-safe protective handling where possible.

7.  Underlying structural stop: exit option at executable marketable
    limit when confirmed structural invalidation occurs; test intrabar
    trigger vs completed-bar trigger separately.

8.  Option loss guard: independently exit if observed executable bid
    implies a hard dollar loss threshold; this protects against option
    decay/IV change before the chart stop.

9.  Profit taking: compare fixed 1.5R, 2R, partial exits, and structure
    targets as mutually exclusive test arms. R must use option dollars
    after simulated costs.

10. Trailing: only after threshold favorable excursion; compare 9EMA
    close-break vs ATR trail; never change a rule in response to
    unrealized P&L without logging.

11. Time stop: test 3, 6 and 9 completed 5m bars with no minimum
    favorable excursion; exact threshold chosen on training data.

12. Mandatory intraday liquidation: schedule well ahead of
    broker-specific expiration interventions; verify flat via broker
    fill/position data, not merely an order request.

8 | Agent state machine and pseudocode

States: DISABLED → DATA_READY → REGIME_CLASSIFIED → CANDIDATE →
CONFIRMED → RISK_APPROVED → ORDER_PENDING → FILLED → MANAGED → CLOSED.
Any safety violation moves to BLOCKED, then requires an explicit reset.

13. On each completed 5m bar: validate timestamps, OHLCV, quotes,
    corporate actions, calendar, and data freshness.

14. Build only historical-to-now indicators; lock opening range after
    9:45; classify regime with no look-ahead.

15. Generate at most one setup-specific directional candidate; evaluate
    required gates; log each gate and rejection.

16. Apply optional RSI / SMT per experimental configuration; do not mix
    rules across backtest variants.

17. Fetch actual option chain/quotes; check eligible contract, spread,
    depth, model risk, portfolio and daily limits.

18. If all pass, submit capped limit order with client order ID; monitor
    fills, partials, cancels and duplicate requests.

19. Manage structural, option loss, profit, trailing, time and session
    exits; reconcile positions with broker.

20. Record immutable market snapshot, signal features, contract
    selection, fills, realized P&L, slippage and errors.

9 | Risk policy — example research limits, not optimized parameters

-   Per trade: cap worst-case modeled loss at 0.25–0.50% of account
    equity; also cap total option premium paid. Never increase risk
    after a losing trade.

-   Daily stop: suspend entries after 1–1.5% of account equity in
    realized plus adverse marked open loss, or 3 consecutive full-risk
    losses; use the more conservative bound.

-   Correlated exposure: aggregate SPY, QQQ and correlated
    technology-stock directional deltas; do not count them as fully
    independent trades.

-   Operational controls: max positions, max orders/minute, retry
    throttling, kill switch, broker reconciliation, alerting,
    duplicate-order protection, API credential isolation, and manual
    override.

-   Margin/day-trading rules: as of 2026 the new FINRA intraday-margin
    framework is effective, with transition permitted for brokers
    through October 20, 2027. Confirm the actual brokerage
    implementation and account eligibility [3–4].

-   Expiration: options can be exercised under OCC procedures, and
    broker deadlines or forced liquidations differ; verify your broker’s
    exact procedures, including any early-liquidation cutoffs [5–6].

10 | Backtest and validation acceptance checklist

21. Write immutable, versioned definitions of all signals, fill
    assumptions, eligible universe, trading dates, fees and risk limits
    before examining final holdout data.

22. Use survivorship-aware historical underlying bars plus historical
    option NBBO/quotes, actual strikes/expiries, dividends, splits,
    halts and corporate actions; do not backtest option P&L from the
    underlying only.

23. Use chronological train → validation → untouched holdout and rolling
    walk-forward tests; enforce adequate embargo around overlapping
    trades and adjacent labels.

24. Compare strategy A/B/C/D individually, and 1DTE against longer
    expiries. Include no-SMT/no-RSI baselines and a simplistic ORB
    benchmark.

25. Model entries at realistic ask/marketable-limit fills and exits at
    bids, including variable slippage, commissions, partial fills,
    rejects, latency, price impact and quote staleness.

26. Report after-cost expectancy per trade in $, R and %; profit factor,
    drawdown, win rate, payoff ratio, trade count, tail loss, exposure,
    execution failure and performance by month/regime/symbol.

27. Stress-test twice the normal slippage, wider spreads, missed fills,
    ±1 bar latency, low-liquidity event days and parameter
    perturbations. Inspect sensitivity rather than optimizing one
    precise threshold.

28. Paper trade with live-time quotes and broker confirmations. Require
    stable risk monitoring and reconciliation before even considering
    small-size, supervised production.

Suggested promotion gate (policy proposal, not scientific guarantee):
positive out-of-sample net expectancy with uncertainty intervals
excluding a materially negative edge; sufficient independent trades
across regimes; no dependence on one ticker/month; under-stress drawdown
inside risk budget; operational incidents resolved. If any condition
fails, remain PAPER ONLY.
